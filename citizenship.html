Overview

A second passport, backed by a real asset

Türkiye grants citizenship to foreign nationals who buy real estate worth at least USD 400,000 and keep it for three years. There's no residency requirement and no language test, and your spouse and children under 18 qualify on the same application. We source the property, secure the official valuation and handle every filing through to passport collection.

No residency requirement

You don't need to live in Türkiye before or after you apply.

Whole family included

Your spouse and children under 18 apply alongside you.

Dual citizenship allowed

You keep your current nationality.

An asset that works

Rental income and capital growth while you hold the property.

Questions

Frequently asked

Ask an advisor
Can I buy more than one property to reach USD 400,000?

Yes. Several properties can be combined, provided their total official valuation meets the threshold and all of them are bought within the program rules.

Do I need to visit Türkiye?

Not necessarily. Most steps can be handled through a power of attorney. Many clients still visit once for viewings and biometrics.

Can I sell the property later?

Yes, once the three-year holding period ends. Your citizenship is not affected by the sale.

What are the costs beyond the property price?

Expect title deed tax, valuation, legal and translation fees. We give you a full cost breakdown before you commit.

Which other programs do you offer?

The Turkish Residence Permit, the UAE Golden Visa, the Greek Golden Visa, Cyprus Permanent Residency and investor routes in Indonesia and the UK.
